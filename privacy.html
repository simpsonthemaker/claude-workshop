Privacy

Short version: I collect your name and email so I can send you a Zoom link. I don't sell it, and I don't pass it to your brokerage.

Who this is

This site and the workshop email list are run by Sidney's Catalogue Group, David Simpson, in Calgary, Alberta.

This page covers this website and the workshop list only. It does not cover real estate brokerage services, which sit with Real Broker.

What gets collected

When you sign up for a workshop:

  • Your name and email address, because you typed them in
  • Which link you arrived on, so I know which brokerage or session you came from
  • Whether you ticked the newsletter box, and when

That's it. No tracking pixels, no advertising cookies, nothing that follows you around afterwards.

What it gets used for

  • Sending you the Zoom link and anything you need for the session you registered for
  • The newsletter, only if you ticked the box. If you didn't, you hear from me about the workshop and nothing else
  • Knowing which sessions worked. Which link you came from tells me that, in aggregate

Registering is not consent to a newsletter. They are two separate things and the tick box is what separates them. The box is never ticked for you, and it is never required to attend.

The rules behind this

Two pieces of Canadian law shape how this works.

  • CASL, Canada's anti-spam legislation, is why the newsletter box exists and why it is never ticked for you. Signing up for a workshop is consent to hear about that workshop. Ongoing email is a separate yes
  • Alberta's PIPA, the provincial law on personal information, is why you can ask what I hold on you and have it corrected. Deleting it on request goes further than the law asks, and I do that anyway

Who else sees it

  • Kit, the email service that stores the list and sends the emails. Their privacy policy
  • Zoom, if you attend a session, on the same terms as joining any Zoom call

Nobody else. It is not sold, not rented, not traded, and not handed to the brokerage that hosted the session. If a broker invites their team, the broker does not get the list of who signed up.

Getting out

  • Unsubscribe using the link at the bottom of any email. It works immediately
  • Change your mind about the newsletter without leaving the workshop list, or the other way round. Just ask
  • Have it deleted entirely. Email me and it's gone, not archived

How long it's kept

For as long as you're on the list. When you unsubscribe, Kit keeps a record that you did, so you don't get added back by accident. Ask for deletion and that goes too.

Asking me something

Email info@sidneyscatalogue.com. That includes asking what I hold on you, which you're entitled to do.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it.
